## Limitations and boundaries
- Frontend only: no backend connection, database, migrations, authentication, persistence, payments, secrets, inquiry endpoint or working submissions.
- No publishing or production deployment was performed. No dependencies were added.
- The existing TanStack Start/Vite foundation was preserved; no custom server-side business functionality was added.
- ICK Opportunities remains separate and untouched. Product details/link require verification.
- Contact details and legal content are pending founder approval; placeholders are not legal policies or assurances.
- Google Fonts is an external font request; it is disclosed on the privacy placeholder. Self-hosting fonts may be considered in an approved later phase.
- WCAG 2.2 AA is a target, not a certification. Full automated/manual accessibility auditing and assistive-technology testing were not performed.
- LCP, INP and CLS targets were not benchmarked; production performance, security and hosting compatibility remain unverified.
- Sprint 01 stopped here. Any backend or production work requires explicit authorization.
